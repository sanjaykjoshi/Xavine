# Xavine: fuel log, petrol pumps, parking and reminders for Android Auto

This is a personal Android Auto companion for a Tata Altroz XTA (petrol, 37 L tank).

## On the car screen
- **Log a fill-up** (full tank or partial): enter the amount, litres and odometer. You'll need to be stopped, for example at the pump. It then shows ₹/L and that tank's km/l.
- **Fuel stations near me:** the nearest petrol pumps within 10 km, shown on a map from OpenStreetMap. Tap one to navigate there.
- **Save parking:** stores your parking spot. This also happens automatically when you disconnect from Android Auto.
- **Due-soon reminders** appear at the top of the screen. A reminder counts as due soon when it's within 500 km or 15 days.

## On the phone
- Average and last-tank mileage, this month's spend, cost per km and last price per litre.
- Fill-up history. Long-press a fill-up to delete it.
- **Walk to my car**, which gives Google Maps walking directions to your saved parking spot.
- Reminders for service, insurance, PUC, tyre rotation or anything else, due by odometer and/or date.

## Install
1. Download `Xavine.apk` from **Releases** and install it.
2. Open Xavine and tap **Allow location & notifications**.
3. Turn on Android Auto's **Unknown sources**: in Android Auto settings, tap *Version* 10 times, then open ⋮ → *Developer settings*. The app's **How to show Xavine in Android Auto** button has the step-by-step instructions.

All data stays on your phone. Internet is only used to look up fuel stations.
